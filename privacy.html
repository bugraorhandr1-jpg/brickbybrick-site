Privacy Policy

BrickByBrick Uploader. Last updated: November 15, 2026.

BrickByBrick Uploader is a private tool that the BrickByBrick channel uses to upload and share its own videos. It is not offered to the public and has a single user: the channel owner. It only ever accesses the owner's own accounts.

YouTube

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the tool uses YouTube API Services with three permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos and set their thumbnails.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm that it is connected to the BrickByBrick channel and to check the processing and privacy status of the videos it uploaded.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own view and audience-retention reports, so the owner can see which videos work. No revenue data is read.

TikTok

With the owner's permission, given through TikTok's own login page, the tool uses two TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic: to confirm which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send the channel's own videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app; the tool never posts publicly.

How the data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, and are sent only to Google or TikTok to make the calls described above.
  • The tool keeps a local log of its uploads (video ID, file checksum, title and status) and the channel's own analytics reports on the owner's computer.
  • The owner runs the tool with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), which can see upload results such as video IDs, statuses and the channel's own view counts. It never sees the sign-in tokens.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access and data

The owner can delete the locally stored tokens and logs at any time, can remove the tool's access to the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can remove its access to the TikTok account in the TikTok app's security settings.

Platform terms

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the BrickByBrick channel on YouTube.
